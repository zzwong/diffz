## Rust dependencies

The `gpui-kit` crates (Apache-2.0) bring [GPUI](https://github.com/zed-industries/zed)
into diffz. The rest of the dependencies are the crates recorded in `Cargo.lock`,
each with its own license. `about.toml` and `deny.toml` drive `cargo about` and
`cargo deny`, which produce a complete license inventory for distribution-ready binaries.
